The South Road Journal- June 24, Year 3

CUBE OF TOBIAS- WONDER OF TOPS

Heliastes, SRJ Editor-in-Chief

   It sounds incredible; a geometrically perfect cube, placed on an equally flat plane in the middle of seemingly nowhere. But the Cube of Tobias is a real place, formed naturally over the eons by wind erosion and plane cleavage processes. "Many people mistakenly believe that the Cube has to be man-made because it's cubic form is perceived to be unnatural," says pepevog, professor of geology at TOPS University. "However, cubes form naturally quite often, especially in crystals. On close inspection, it's clear that the Cube of Tobias is one giant crystalline structure whose surrounding sediment were eroded away over time."
   Not much surrounds the Cube of Tobias, aside from a small settlement mostly built around a gift shop and a small travel essentials store for visitors. "Not much happens here in Nadiya," says Bonenaut7, sitting in a gazebo while a light rain drizzled. "People drop by to see the Cube, then they buy a key chain in the gift shop and head out." After chewing on a spring of rye for a bit then spitting it out, he continued, "Yup, not much happens around here." A tumbleweed blew down the sole street in town.
   "Yeah, I came by to see it because all brochures said it was a wonder of the world," said beanburritos42, meteorite enthusiast. "But when I saw it, it was just kind of meh to be honest considering the trip out here." 0xide_z3r0, also visiting, chimed in, "Yeah I mean, I work with cubes day-in and day-out, why's a cube 20x bigger worth coming out for?" Bonenaut7 agreed: "Yeah I'd leave if I could, but then some hooligans would just show up and vandalize the thing; probably turn it into a hill or something dumb."
