The South Road Journal- October 1, Year 4

SOUTH ROAD JOURNAL ACQUIRED

Heliastes, SRJ Editor-in-Chief

   Well folks, it's been a good run but, unfortunately, all good things come to an end. The South Road Journal, TOPS's preferred journal of record for the past four years, will cease operations under it's own name in the coming days. The SRJ has been acquired by TwoNutsHeim Publishing, a well-established publication house. Some SRJ staff will transfer over to their flagship newspaper "The West Street Journal". Others will promptly lose their jobs. It's unfortunate but we have the following words of encouragement for former staff writers: Last paycheck comes in on Friday, please vacate the premises immediately.
   With that said, viewers should feel comforted in the narrowing of reading options to essentially the only newspaper left on TOPS. The West Street Journal has a long storied tradition of writing what viewers want to see: stories about economic class mobility and what's happening with the rich famous, i.e. the people that really matter. Thus, there should be no substantial changes to the quality of stories received when SRJ subscribership is forcibly bumped over to the West Street Journal.
   On a personal note, as the Editor-in-Chief of the South Road Journal since conception, it's been a deep pleasure serving everyone and I look forward to my exciting new position as Editor-in-Chief at the West Street Journal. I look forward to providing my full journalistic enthusiasm with full editorial authority and no meddling from TwoNutsHeim Publishing whatsoever. The fact that I received a huge RG payout and a huge mansion on main street will not affect my discretion whatsoever and I look forward to your continued support!

Most Sincerely,
Heliastes, SRJ Editor-in-Chief, Future WSJ Editor-in-Chief
