The South Road Journal- September 18, Year 2

NO PERCH IN PERCH POINT

Heliastes, SRJ Editor-in-Chief

   Walking down the picturesque boardwalks in downtown Perch Point arching over pristine blue waters, one could imagine the joy of early settlers when they cast their first lines out from the shores. These days though, the namesake fish of Perch Point is nowhere to be found, a victim of both overfishing and habitat fragmentation. Studies show that natural competitors of perch, such as white crappie and pickerel, have grown to dominate the Perch Point ecosystem as the town exponentially expanded since Year 0. As a result, perch can only be found in niche corners of the lake, pushed to the edges by selective pressures.
   Local officials deny that there's a lack of perch within the county, describing the study results as "flawed and lacking credible evidence."

Other citizens of Perch Point had their own views on the matter. "Iono perch is mid NGL" said one youngster. "Actually, I wouldn't mind the town being renamed Crappie Point" said a local elder. Regardless, it seems for now that the only perch visible in Perch Point is the one engraved in the town sign.
