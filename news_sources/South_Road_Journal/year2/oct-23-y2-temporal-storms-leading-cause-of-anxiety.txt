TEMPORAL STORMS LEADING CAUSE OF ANXIETY

Heliastes, SRJ Editor-in-Chief

   Scientists associated with TOPS University School of Temporal Mechanics and the Dave Institute today released their multi-year study on the effect of temporal storms on mental well-being. The findings indicate that many Vintagians suffer significant anxiety as a result of experiencing temporal storms. While heavy temporal storms impart the severest effect, even minor temporal storms can significantly impair mental states, impairing workplace function and family cohesion.
   lilyniru, lead author of the study, stated that while it was common sense temporal storms were stressful, the extent of the damage to the economy and society as a whole was not known.

"Previously, it was just accepted as a part of life that temporal storms came and went and occasionally warped our loved ones into slimy grotesque rust abominations. With these new results, we can move past the stigmatizing effects of temporal storms and learn to accept and treat those most affected by it's devastating consequences." The study also demonstrated that certain common medications, including aqua regia and blue meanies, could greatly increase the amount of stress experienced during a storm. "Vintagians with substance abuse issues fared far worse during storms than those in control groups, often inflicting serious physical harm onto themselves through falls off cliffs or walking into operating furnaces due to the exacerbating effects of drugs during storms."
   Members of the public reacted to the study with varying emotions, ranging from indifference to hope. Bykikon, a granary specialist, said, "They're just storms; I just do what my parents and grandparents before always did, dig a hole in the dirt, cover myself with dirt, and hope for the best." Grand_Paladin, bridge engineer, commented that the study would lead them to reflect on the amount of alcohol consumed. "I guess ten liters of booze per storm is probably too much; I'll try to cut it back to only five, maybe six liters."
