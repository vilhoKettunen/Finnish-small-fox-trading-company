The South Road Journal- July 30, Year 2

TYRON CALLS FOR RATE HIKES, MARKETS CRASH

Heliastes, SRJ Editor-in-Chief

   For the first time in TOPS history, Tyron, leader of the official world and Chair of the TOPS Reserve Board, announced late Friday afternoon that the TOPS target rate would need to significantly increase to address unchecked currency inflation. Rampant rusty gear printing perpetuated by certain anti-government factions, so-called 'mobfarms', were said to be the key cause of the inflation spike. Said Tyron, "TOPS Economic Recession incoming" followed by a "kek" emote.
   After hours markets dropped sharply upon the unexpected news announcement with certain sectors such as linen cloth stacks and meme chiseled goods falling 30-40%. Consumer cyclicals including flax grain and foraged foods rose significantly on the news as panic buying of essential goods continued into early Saturday morning.
   Some TOPS citizens reacted to the news with dismay and alarm, calling the interest rate hikes yet another blow to their savings, citing astronomical transportation costs and rampant trader deposit scams as more dire issues facing the economy. According to Raistrand, local economics professor at TOPS-U, "Removing mobfarms would more likely break the whole AH because of the lack of liquidity."
  Others indicated that the interest rate hikes were painful but necessary for the RG currency to recover. Said Mordenator, regional farmer, "Mob farms need to get cooked, it sucks when some players have infinite gear supply with little effort." Regardless, TOPS economy will likely continue to roil in the coming weeks.
