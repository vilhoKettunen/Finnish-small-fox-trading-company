MIRACLE APPETITE-ENHANCING DRUG CHANGES LIVES

Heliastes, SRJ Editor-in-Chief

   Only a few weeks ago, such a development would have been though impossible. These days though, any local tavern or cafe is packed with Vintagians eating all the pies and stews they can cram in their faces. "I used to only be able to eat one, maybe two pies a day," said Monkaka, road constructor, "but now I easily can wolf down five or six different pies, it's great!" Vintagians agree; quality of life has greatly improved now that they aren't limited to choosing between the delicious foods they crave; they can have it all!
  The key behind this breakthrough development is the new blockbuster drug Vakgovy. Vakgovy works by combining two key mechanisms in one formulation: a mitochondrial decoupler that increases metabolic rate and a cathartic that expedites digestive motility. "I developed Vakgovy mainly to bump up my food sales... I guess also for humanity and stuff", said Vak, developer of Vakgovy. What's in Vakgovy? Vak explains, "I pretty much just dump all my extra flax grain into linseed oil pills and slap the label on." Vakgovy users see a marked increase in their food uptake nearly immediately upon use with minimum side effects (extreme fever, liver damage, sudden death syndrome, etc.).
   "My doctor tells me that my new diet fulfills the Food Pentangle perfectly (Fruits, Vegetables, Grains, Protein, Diary) and my health has never been better!" says bonenaut7, regional warehouse manager. Truly, a new golden age of TOPS has begun. One only wonders: can the food supply keep up?
